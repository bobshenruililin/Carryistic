# E3 · A score that follows the light

Five 90-second chapter cues are in `public/motion/` and in the private score archive. They are an editorial shortlist for Opus’s review. The 38-second audition plays the cues in room order. The private score archive has all 20 takes and the signal measurements. | Night | C | *Dusk til Dawn* through the flame in *Artful and Grateful* | The bass alternates B-flat and D. The audition leaves Night during its D bar and enters the first sustained D in Gold. |
| Gold | B | *Domus Dei* and *To Bleed in Liquid Gold* | D minor reads clearly in the pitch profile. There is no strongly measurable backbeat. Let the pad release under the start of the record-store beat. |
| Neon | D | The record store and the graphic rooms | This take has the strongest 84 BPM pulse of the Neon set. Its later section settles around D, with some major and minor color. The audition enters there. |
| Day | C | *Fly*, DC, New York and Italy | A clear 84 BPM pulse and D-major pitch profile. Use a one-beat fade as the starburst opens on the waterfront. A longer overlap would expose the change of third. |
| Studio | C | RISD and *Proud Daddy* | D major, with fewer strong transients than the other two D-major Studio takes. Give the final view of Carry beside the painting time to settle. |

The audition uses twelve beats from each cue on the 84 BPM grid. Night into Gold, Gold into Neon and Day into Studio use two-beat equal-power overlaps of 1.43 seconds. Neon into Day uses a 0.71-second linear overlap. The visitor’s scroll can continue to drive the picture at their own pace. Keep the sound toggle off at entry, and pause the score when an original film plays with its own sound.

All five MP3 exports retain the complete take and use 192 kbps stereo. Gain matching brought every stem to between −23.00 and −23.02 LUFS. The loudest true peak across the stems is −3.35 dBTP. The audio keeps the original tempo and pitch. Every original take was generated with the instrumental setting and screened with local voice activity and speech recognition. Those checks returned zero candidate speech segments and zero words across all 20. Listening approval is still needed for vocal-like timbre, particularly the sustained Gold pad, and for the harmonic joins. The D-centered profiles for Night and Neon include related chords and modal color, so those two picks still need a tonal check by ear.
