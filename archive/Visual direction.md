## The gesture survives the edit

In Fly, four versions of a red-hooded figure fall from upper left to lower right. The waterfront disappears on the next slide. Then the body disappears too, and four increasingly large colored squares still describe the fall. That little sequence is the clearest statement of Carry’s approach. [P05-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wp2VFwDsD8UG5DhtBlPqF6HDyUj0m1sC/view?usp=drivesdk), [P05-02](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16AdpWEhbLbXWSAALcZGeTG3mNH6LNzQ_/view?usp=drivesdk), [P05-03](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1IutMGiEvXfEh0Wew_AJav-0sm9TXU3MC/view?usp=drivesdk).

There is the same playfulness in the record-store work, with a human reason to care about the trick. Someone pulls a cap down with both hands. Once you have seen the photograph, a few red silhouettes can repeat the whole gesture. The oil portraits offer a quieter pleasure. You can find exactly which glance or grip the artist chose to keep. [P11-04](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Pqt3w1RDthUdaO8Mhtd6rfniLfuPi2FR/view?usp=drivesdk), [P10-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15MPVlafPt57Z879mu6oRs1CFaq1yedJq/view?usp=drivesdk), [P01-09](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lI6H_oGDl0mrHyHiu4soLdYIfnL72GDU/view?usp=drivesdk), [P01-02](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HkmADw72mzClh_RoRjr3SulZoH2JJ-BZ/view?usp=drivesdk), [P07-04](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uuStNGpGtGbRT42tLOFLPSa9egTbPZ_5/view?usp=drivesdk), [P07-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VkUIPAGckBFs1vlvHkvLQTH_M1E-gn6q/view?usp=drivesdk).

## What should lead

Lead with one person and a readable action. For the portfolio entrance, use [P05-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wp2VFwDsD8UG5DhtBlPqF6HDyUj0m1sC/view?usp=drivesdk) at its original aspect ratio. The diagonal reads even at thumbnail size. Let [P05-02](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16AdpWEhbLbXWSAALcZGeTG3mNH6LNzQ_/view?usp=drivesdk) and [P05-03](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1IutMGiEvXfEh0Wew_AJav-0sm9TXU3MC/view?usp=drivesdk) reveal what Carry did to it. [P01-02](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HkmADw72mzClh_RoRjr3SulZoH2JJ-BZ/view?usp=drivesdk) makes a slower alternative entrance when the piece is presented as a painting portfolio.

Give the photographs and process shots room beside the finished pieces. [P11-04](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Pqt3w1RDthUdaO8Mhtd6rfniLfuPi2FR/view?usp=drivesdk) belongs beside [P10-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15MPVlafPt57Z879mu6oRs1CFaq1yedJq/view?usp=drivesdk). [P07-04](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uuStNGpGtGbRT42tLOFLPSa9egTbPZ_5/view?usp=drivesdk) belongs beside [P07-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VkUIPAGckBFs1vlvHkvLQTH_M1E-gn6q/view?usp=drivesdk). Keep the horizon, hands and angle of the head aligned long enough for the viewer to discover the correspondence.

Save the strongest glints and textures for close inspection. The silver mask under the mirrored hat, the piled red beads and the graphite heads each reward a pause. White margins, colored borders and tiny insets are part of these compositions. Preserve them in the wide view. [P02-06](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eGyLasS788eJUZ03eVqOsH_M5jx5YMEm/view?usp=drivesdk), [P08-03](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15rFkuQTQdiGKoBkTz2f93kOKlBo7WUZc/view?usp=drivesdk), [P12-03](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uA23dXyfPNjSRNRyz8ADiOA2HXAfr3zI/view?usp=drivesdk), [P04-05](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wGYW6GRQxr0M4PPGpGc_5nVvuNkePO0M/view?usp=drivesdk).

## A 28-second portfolio film

0–4 s  |  Find the gaze. Start on the photographic portrait, register the eye and jaw against the pencil drawing, then cut to the blue-and-orange oil. Hold the full portrait for one beat. [P01-09](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lI6H_oGDl0mrHyHiu4soLdYIfnL72GDU/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P01-08](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1C-dPoW4NA2nFNCVhXAbIi9Rw8OSES_LF/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P01-02](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HkmADw72mzClh_RoRjr3SulZoH2JJ-BZ/view?usp=drivesdk).

4–9 s  |  Give the figure room. Cut wide to the waterfront. Reveal the four existing red-hooded positions down the diagonal, then drop the background to the white cutout composition. Use the four photographed poses as the movement. [P05-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wp2VFwDsD8UG5DhtBlPqF6HDyUj0m1sC/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P05-02](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16AdpWEhbLbXWSAALcZGeTG3mNH6LNzQ_/view?usp=drivesdk).

9–12 s  |  Let the pose disappear. Match each cutout to the square in its place. Preserve the increasing scale and land on the large blue block. Hold just long enough to recognize the fall again. [P05-02](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16AdpWEhbLbXWSAALcZGeTG3mNH6LNzQ_/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P05-03](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1IutMGiEvXfEh0Wew_AJav-0sm9TXU3MC/view?usp=drivesdk).

12–17 s  |  Bring the person back. Use the blue block as the color cut into the jersey. Register the red cap and both hands across the photo and graphic. Bring the existing red echoes in from left to right while the first figure holds still. [P11-04](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Pqt3w1RDthUdaO8Mhtd6rfniLfuPi2FR/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P10-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15MPVlafPt57Z879mu6oRs1CFaq1yedJq/view?usp=drivesdk).

17–22 s  |  Change the material. Cut to the pair of records held over the face. Match the round center label to one gold-rimmed opening in the heart sculpture, then drift down the beaded strand. [P11-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QRt2sGY41FluuodaLPH7-rNFY39TySU2/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P08-04](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1z2EzARp_2717IRGHn2urMzlvNMAFktdQ/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P08-03](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15rFkuQTQdiGKoBkTz2f93kOKlBo7WUZc/view?usp=drivesdk).

22–28 s  |  Show the hand that made it. Match a red bead to the orange basketball, switch from the source photograph to the painted child, then pull wide to the person holding the canvas. Give that last view two seconds. [P07-04](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uuStNGpGtGbRT42tLOFLPSa9egTbPZ_5/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P07-01](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VkUIPAGckBFs1vlvHkvLQTH_M1E-gn6q/view?usp=drivesdk) → [P07-02](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lwPZTkXnrpBDhuk4hJ030nkl6xnJxyPX/view?usp=drivesdk).

## How to use the moving references

The Blackout Awaits carries a piece of graphic design through physical space. Compare the paper above the door around 15 s with the cluster of posters around 59 s. In the sculpture clip, the changing angle reveals the cavities while the bead strands stay anchored. Use its real camera move for material studies. [P03-01.mp4](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_9_fW2AjEPFGm5NRlqEgUC_u9FRSVWl4/view?usp=drivesdk), [P08-02.mp4](https://drive.google.com/file/d/13-DeUhXYAUrHozaD9paJTDWBr9t1NCXn/view?usp=drivesdk), [P08-07.mp4](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HOMqQ8YsjLGYTjt5lPui4yQ2Ou5J-it8/view?usp=drivesdk).

## Prompt for Opus

“Use this atlas and the linked source files to turn the 28-second cut into a six-shot treatment. For each shot give the input IDs, start frame, end frame, fixed visual landmark, movement, duration and cut cue. Use compositing for the existing cutouts, tints and repeats. Where you propose generated movement, identify it and say which visible detail must stay fixed. Preserve the artwork’s gaze, hand placement, collage borders and original aspect ratio. Return a shot list an editor could assemble.”
