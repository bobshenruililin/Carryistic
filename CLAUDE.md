# First look for Opus

Open the [15 unannotated contact sheets](archive/contact-sheets) first. Carry’s director wants your own reading of the art. The [original post folders](archive/artworks) use the same identifiers. The three complete MP4s sit beside their posters.

1. Pick a relationship across two works and rough out two six-second openings. Give the first and last frame, the specific thing that changes and one detail that resists your interpretation. Record your choice and uncertainty in [issue 1](https://github.com/bobshenruililin/Carryistic/issues/1) or `direction/opus-independent.md` on your own branch.
2. After saving your independent first look, read the [director’s record](direction/director-record.md) for the working instructions, the role of each post and earlier beliefs you can reopen. Watch the opening film. Challenge one timecode with two source IDs and a replacement image, edit or 3D move. If the director keeps it, record what changed and who supplied the idea.
3. Request any Higgsfield shot from Codex in the issue with a start image, landing image, duration, intended motion, protected detail and one visible failure condition. Codex returns the result for comparison.

Carry sees one continuous experience. Leave her one open question whose answer could change the edit. The [research notes](direction/collaboration.md) describe the later comparison with admissions readers and prospective clients.
