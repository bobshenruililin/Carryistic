# Three ways to move through a picture

## Calista Suherman · @cal1star · 8.47 s

A woman enters from the left against black. A hillside path is already visible in a rounded window on the right. Around 1.7–2.4 s her legs and shoes overlap the window’s lower edge. By 2.5 s both feet are inside. For the remaining six seconds she walks away on the path while the window holds its size. Her diminishing figure tells us how deep the image is. The caption credits @joelroac as inspiration. [Original reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbKYg-LMPiK/)

**For Carry.** In *Fly*, try letting one red hood cross the waterfront print’s edge before it settles into its published position. Hold the horizon steady so Carry can judge the crossing. Her four poses already supply the path. [Waterfront and cutouts, P05-01–03](../archive/artworks/P05%20-%20Fly)

## Minta · @potato0x_x · reel captioned 😝 · 16.89 s

At 1.17 s a runner raises a yellow sheet toward the lens. By 1.5 s hand-drawn lips occupy its center. The sheet’s edges shift slightly as the mouth changes shape, and strips of street remain visible on either side. At 5.30 s the cut returns to the face. Later, a tilted street mirror enters as a small inset around 8.8 s, then fills the frame by 9.2 s. The readable lettering near the finish belongs to the vending machine in the shot. [Original reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZZzAn0Mrd3/)

**For Carry.** Let a visitor lift the actual *BLACK OUT* sheet while a strip of its room stays visible. The letters move with the paper. Stop where the viewer can read the words and still see where the sheet came from. [Carry’s printed sign, P03-01](../archive/artworks/P03%20-%20The%20Blackout%20Awaits)

## Carlos · @crlos.mp4 · *Eternity* · 8.01 s

In the first second, luminous patterns occupy a head and shoulders against white. Around 3.5–4.4 s, a raised-hand figure shifts from cyan marbling to a dark silhouette against the same blue halo. From about 5–7 s the figure is small beneath a broad blue sky, and the gold pattern returns inside the body. At 8.01 s the last frame is a closed eyelid. Carlos credits Airmow’s *FRAGMENTS* and cites vinc.visions as inspiration. [Original reel and credits](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdRkI5hMu64/?hl=en)

**For Carry.** Her yellow profile and photographic face already share the glasses and outline in P09. Keep the glasses registered as the image inside that outline changes. Leave Carlos’s fractal textures and the reference soundtracks with their makers. [Graphic pose studies, P09-03 and P09-08](../archive/artworks/P09%20-%20Graphic%20pose%20studies)
