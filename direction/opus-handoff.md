# Direction pass for Opus

The public portfolio has one opening film and one directed journey. Use this pass to challenge the cut while Carry and the director can still change it. The archive contains the source frames, complete motion files, a visual atlas and credits. [Project and live experience](../README.md) · [Archive](../archive) · [Reference atlas](../archive/Reference%20atlas.pdf) · [Evidence and measurements](artist-reading.md)

1. Look at P05-01/02/03, P01-09/08/02 and P07-04/01/10 before reading the treatment. Write down the first detail you remember from each group. Then watch the opening and say where your reading diverges.
2. Bring the director one alternate transition. Cite the two source IDs, the exact landmark that survives, the target duration and the first frame in which the proposed movement would be visible. State which part is your invention.
3. If you propose generated video, specify the source and landing frames, the protected features and a failure that would make the editor choose a straight cut. The authored shapes, eyes and hands need to survive the transfer. The photographed camera arc in P08-02 provides a useful material reference.
4. End with one question for Carry that could change the edit. She might describe what the basketball means, reject a first-person title or ask to hold longer on a tool, margin or hand.

Leave a short direction note on a separate branch or the handoff pull request. Include the shot, the source, the proposed change and what would make you abandon it. The director can then test it against the existing cut before making a public revision.
