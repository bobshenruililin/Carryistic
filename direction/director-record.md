# The director’s record

Carry’s work supplies the images. The director wants a visitor to experience how Carry sees and leave able to name a choice she made. *Afterimage* is the team’s interpretation of that work. The dream premise and the Steph Curry connection came from the director. Carry has room to give either a different meaning in her own words.

## Current instructions

- Give the visitor a simple entrance, a wordless film, then a continuous path through the spatial studies and the complete portfolio. Use score, Foley and accessible sound captions. The director ruled out narration. Put alternate cuts in editorial review.
- The director likes the current interactive work and asked for “words to be part of the art.” Use a borderless presentation. Keep the white space Carry drew into a layout, the type on a poster, the number on a jersey and the mode labels on a photographed camera. Place added language where it helps someone read or use the work.
- Give all 15 posts a place. Each of the 153 positions should be individually viewable, with credits and a route to its original. An admissions reader should be able to follow a decision from source to finished work. A prospective client should be able to identify a skill, find the collaborators and reach the artist.
- Carry can remember a view and leave an open written response. She chooses whether to share it. Keep the first-person title open until she supplies or approves the words. Vercel is the director’s requested hosting destination. [Archive index](../archive/Image%20index.csv) · [Project and hosting links](../README.md)
