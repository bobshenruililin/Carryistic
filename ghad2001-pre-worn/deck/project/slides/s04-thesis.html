SPEAKER 1 · 1:30–2:00 “Here is our pair four times, on its way to us: the field, the factory, the ship, Hong Kong. It gets bigger as it gets closer. Then the pair drops out and only the colours are left, because that is how the journey reaches us. Our argument: globalisation made this pair cheap and everywhere by spreading its making, wearing and discarding across borders. Its health costs land where its record stops. We judge it by one test: does the person who bears the health cost have a record of, or a say in, the decision that caused it? We follow the brief’s five sections.
