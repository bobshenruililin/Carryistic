SPEAKER 3 · 4:55–5:30 “Every pale patch on this pair is a process. Factories sand denim by hand, spray it with potassium permanganate, burn it with lasers, or cut and rip it. Levi’s record says our pair was finished and laundered in Türkiye. It doesn’t say how. In Türkiye’s own denim workshops, workers who spray potassium permanganate reported skin and breathing problems. That was a small study of about eight factories, not our pair’s factory, and the estimate is that up to two million people work with the chemical. Fashion buys the look. The worker pays for the method. Park would say two systems are optimising for different things.”
