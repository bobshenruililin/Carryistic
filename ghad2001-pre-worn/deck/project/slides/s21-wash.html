SPEAKER 5 · 12:25–13:15 “In the pollution lecture, Park followed a polyester shirt and its microplastics. We tested that on cotton, and even 100 percent cotton leaves the house. One study found a used pair sheds about fifty thousand indigo fibres in a single wash, and denim made up about a fifth of the microfibres in Arctic sediment samples. Cotton is dyed and treated, so the fibres last. A flow down the drain becomes a stock on the sea floor. Whether that harms health is not established, and we don’t claim it does.
