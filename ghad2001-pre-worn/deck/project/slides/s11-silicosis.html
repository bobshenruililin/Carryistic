SPEAKER 3 · 5:30–6:15 · Let the dots fill before speaking. “Sandblasting was the old way to make denim look worn. These are 74 former denim sandblasters in Turkey, the same people examined twice. In 2007, 41 had silicosis. In 2011, four years after the work had stopped, 71 did, and nine of the original group had already died. Silica dust scars the lung and keeps scarring after the exposure ends. The jeans last a season. The disease lasts a life. In Park’s terms, a flow of dust becomes a stock in the lung.” Source: R09 Akgun et al., Chest 2015. No link to Levi’s or our pair is established.
