SPEAKER 6 · 14:55–16:00 · Let the photo sit for two seconds first. “Many exported clothes reach Kantamanto market in Accra, Ghana: about 15 million garments a week. Before anyone wears them again, someone carries them. These women are kayayei, head porters, mostly young women who moved from the north of Ghana. They carry bales of over 55 kilograms for 30 cents to a dollar a trip. The Or Foundation, an advocacy group, reports lasting spinal harm, and peer-reviewed research describes the same working conditions. About 40 percent of what arrives leaves as waste, into drains and onto beaches. Brooks, whom Park cites, called the second-hand trade a hidden world. Our pair’s next wearer may be in this market, and the person who carries it there certainly is.”
