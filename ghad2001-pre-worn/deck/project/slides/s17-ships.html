SPEAKER 4 · 9:35–10:20 “How does the pair reach us? Levi’s sources from about thirty countries, ships to regional warehouses, then to shops and online orders here. Ships are Hong Kong’s biggest local source of air emissions, and sulphur and particles from ship fuel harm lungs and hearts near ports. Hong Kong made ships switch to cleaner fuel at berth in 2015 and in all its waters in 2019, but the world only capped sulphur at sea in 2020. A city can clean its harbour; the sea lane needs a global rule. That is Park’s scale mismatch. Hong Kong is also where much of this is managed: Crystal International, founded here in 1970, makes denim across Asia and has counted Levi’s as a customer.” Check the exact EPD wording before Monday.
