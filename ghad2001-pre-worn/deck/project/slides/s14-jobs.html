SPEAKER 4 · 7:35–8:15 “So why not just stop buying jeans? Because the same flow carries benefits. In the 1970s, quotas capped how much clothing Hong Kong and Korea could export. A Korean firm, Daewoo, at its limit, trained 130 workers from a Bangladeshi company in Busan, and Bangladesh’s garment industry grew from there. Where garment jobs arrived, girls stayed in school longer and married and had children later. A boycott would hit the people we worry about.
