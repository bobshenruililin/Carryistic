SPEAKER 7 · 17:55–19:05 “So, three stitches, each matched to where the decision sits. First, record the finish. Publish the factory and finishing method for each product code, so someone can check the air those workers breathe. Buyers can fund safer finishing through supplier finance, as Levi’s and the IFC already do. Second, make injury cover permanent. Bangladesh’s pilot should become a national scheme, funded by employers and brands. Measure success by what injured workers still pay out of their own pockets. Third, make producers pay for what comes after. Hong Kong could follow the EU and make clothing producers fund collection and sorting here, so fewer unsellable clothes are shipped to Accra. Measure it in tonnes re-worn against tonnes landfilled. And us? Wash cold, line-dry, repair; Levi’s has a tailor shop in Hong Kong. That cuts the home share of carbon. It does nothing for the factory’s dust, so match the fix to the stage.”
