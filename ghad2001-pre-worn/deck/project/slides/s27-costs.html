SPEAKER 7 (Hansen) · 17:00–17:55 “Three conclusions. One: spreading the pair across about thirty countries is what makes it cheap and everywhere. It also brings jobs and schooling, so the answer is not less trade. Two: globalisation separates the decision from the consequence. Buyers set the price and the deadline; finishers breathe the dust. We pay at the till; porters carry the bales. The costs sit in other people’s accounts: workers’ lungs, Bangladesh’s clinics, Accra’s drains. They never appear on the price. Three: where nothing is recorded, nothing gets checked. The trail went dark exactly where the harm is. In one pair of jeans, six of this course’s flows cross: goods, people, a pathogen shock, ideas through advertising, pollution, and forced labour.” Visual: the squares from the opening return. The biggest is the till, where we are; the costs shrink as they move away from us.
