SPEAKER 3 · 6:15–7:00 “So the industry fixed it. Turkey banned sandblasting in 2009, and campaigners saw the work move to countries with weaker rules. Levi’s banned it in 2010. In 2016 Levi’s gave away its water-saving finishing methods to competitors. By 2018 a laser could finish a pair in about 90 seconds. Each fix exposed the next constraint. A ban is a policy, not clean lungs. A shared method doesn’t buy the machine. So in 2021 the IFC and Levi’s began pricing supplier loans on social and environmental scores. That is Park’s diffusion point: circulation is not effect. And for our pair, the method isn’t recorded.”
