SPEAKER 4 · 8:15–9:05 “When Rana Plaza collapsed in 2013 and killed 1,134 people, Bangladesh had no employment injury insurance. A pilot scheme started only in 2022. This is Fahim. In 2024 he lost a leg after a road collision on a work errand. It took four institutions to get him back to work: a central fund paid once, the injury pilot pays a monthly pension, a rehabilitation centre fitted a prosthetic leg, and his employer took him back. And insurance is not the same as cover. A study of Bangladeshi garment workers found insured workers saw trained doctors more often, but their out-of-pocket costs were not significantly lower. Seeing a doctor is not the same as being covered: circulation is not effect.”
