R21 Directive (EU) 2025/1892 · R19 · R20 · Levi’s SecondHand (US) · our extrapolation on pre-ripped pairs

SPEAKER 6 · 16:00–17:00 “Putting jeans in a bin is not the same as recycling them. At the sorting table a pair takes one of three roads. It can be worn again, if it is in good condition and someone wants it. It can become fibre: our pair is 100 percent cotton, which helps, but the rivets and buttons have to be cut out first. Or it is residual waste, which someone has to handle safely and pay for. One extrapolation of ours: a pair ripped before it was sold may have less wear left when it reaches the table. The EU now makes clothing producers pay for collection and sorting, with schemes due by 2028. Levi’s runs a buy-back scheme in the US. Hong Kong has neither rule.”
