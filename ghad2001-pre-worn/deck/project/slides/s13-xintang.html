SPEAKER 3 · 7:00–7:35 “Next door to us, in the Pearl River Delta, the town of Xintang called itself the jeans capital of the world, making a few hundred million pairs a year. When Greenpeace sampled the water and sediment around it, 17 of these 21 samples contained heavy metals. Follow the path Park teaches: source, transport, exposure. Dye and metals leave the factory, travel in the river and end up in farmland and fish, and in the people who eat them. Our pair was finished in Türkiye, so this is not its factory. It is what the industry around us has done.” Confirm the Greenpeace report title and Xintang’s distance from Hong Kong.
