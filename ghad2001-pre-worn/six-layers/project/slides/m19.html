OceansAsia, “Masks on the Beach”, Dec 2020 · Soko Islands survey, Feb 2020 · estimates, not measurements

SPEAKER 5 · 10:00–10:50 “The reason Hong Kong wanted a reusable mask is out there. In February 2020 the group OceansAsia found 70 masks on one small beach on the Soko Islands, south of Lantau. Masks had only been in heavy use for a few weeks. Their year-end estimate: 52 billion masks made worldwide in 2020, and if just 3 percent escaped, 1.56 billion reached the sea. That is 4,680 to 6,240 tonnes of polypropylene, which they estimate could take centuries to break down. These are estimates, not measurements, and we say so. Park’s pollution lecture applies directly: a flow of masks becomes a stock of plastic.”
