The prize-winning design changed before it reached our faces.

2018

Gold medal, Geneva. Micro-copper plus a magnetic field. 20 washes.

2020

Magnetic field dropped as “not sustainable”. 60 washes. A new US patent filed.

WHO OWNS IT

HKRITA, from HK$1.28 million of public research money

LCQ15, 20 May 2020 · image: Hong Kong Government, 5 May 2020

SPEAKER 2 · 3:00–3:45 “This is knowledge as a product. The design won a gold medal in Geneva in 2018. It used micro-copper and a magnetic field, and it lasted 20 washes. By 2020 the magnetic field had been dropped as ‘not sustainable’, the mask lasted 60 washes, and HKRITA filed a new patent in the United States. The research cost HK$1.5 million, HK$1.28 million of it public money, and the institute owns the patent. Park’s question about health knowledge applies directly: who creates it, who controls it, and who benefits. Here the public paid for the research and received the masks free. The patent stayed with the institute.”
