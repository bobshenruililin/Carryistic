SPEAKER 5 · 11:45–12:40 “The brief asks whether use looks the same everywhere. It doesn’t. Hong Kong had one of the world’s longest mask mandates: 945 days, from July 2020 to March 2023. On the first day without it, most people kept wearing one. A century earlier, after the 1918 flu, San Francisco had a mask law and an Anti-Mask League fighting it. The same object can be a habit, an order or a protest. In East Asia, masks became normal after SARS and then turned into fashion: pop idols, designer masks, masks matched to outfits. Park’s point about ideas spreading: what people do with an object depends on trust and habit as much as on evidence.”
