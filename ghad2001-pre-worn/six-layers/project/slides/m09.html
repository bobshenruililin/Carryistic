SPEAKER 3 · 3:45–4:25 “Who sewed it? Crystal International, a Hong Kong garment maker whose customers include big fashion brands, made most of the masks, partly in Hong Kong and partly in Vietnam, where the line was in mass production by April 2020. Old Hong Kong textile firms lent workshops and a clean room. That is globalisation in miniature: the brand is Hong Kong, the hands are partly Vietnamese. And notice what the record leaves out. It names the companies, but says nothing about the workers who sewed ten million masks in a pandemic, or their health. The dashed box is our extrapolation, not a record.”
