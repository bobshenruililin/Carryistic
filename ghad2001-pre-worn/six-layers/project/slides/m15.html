SPEAKER 5 · 10:55–11:45 “Do masks work at all? The biggest real-world test is a trial in Bangladesh: 600 villages and about 342,000 adults, randomly assigned. Free masks plus reminders raised mask-wearing from about 13 to 42 percent. Surgical masks cut symptomatic COVID by about 11 percent, and by about 35 percent among people aged 60 and over. The effect of cloth masks was smaller and less certain. Two lessons for CuMask+. It is a reusable cloth mask that passed a surgical-mask filtration standard on paper, so we cannot assume either result. And the trial’s first effect was on behaviour. The protection depended on the seventh layer: people wearing it.” Check the trial’s cloth-mask result before Monday. The next slide (the crowd) is Speaker 5’s too.
