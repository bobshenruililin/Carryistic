SPEAKER 1 · 1:35–2:15 “Last time we tried this with a pair of jeans, the trail went dark. This one is different. Because legislators asked, the government named every company: copper fibre from Jerusalem, fabrics from Hong Kong firms, sewing in Hong Kong and Vietnam, sterilising in a clean room, testing in the United States, delivery by Hongkong Post. It is the best-documented garment we found. And it still leaves out the one thing that decides whether it protects anyone: whether we wore it.”
