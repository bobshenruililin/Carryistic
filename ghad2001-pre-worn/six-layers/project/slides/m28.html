SPEAKER 7 · 15:25–16:25 “Step back. Park’s course follows seven flows, and five of them cross in this one object. Pathogens: SARS taught Hong Kong the habit, and COVID emptied the shelves. Infodemics: a true fact about copper on hard surfaces became an untested claim about a mask. Health knowledge: public research money, a patent held by the institute, and masks given away free. Accountability: no tender, and supplier names blacked out until a legislator asked. Pollution: a flow of masks became a stock of plastic in the sea. The two flows that don’t cross are food and migration, and we won’t force them. The connections matter because each one changed whether the mask protected anyone.”
