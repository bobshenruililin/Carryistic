SPEAKER 3 · 6:35–7:30 “How was it bought? Directly, without an open tender. The rules allow that ‘under extreme urgency’, and after February’s queues the urgency was real. Each mask cost about 40 Hong Kong dollars, and 800 million was set aside for reusable masks. But speed has a cost too. The names of the material suppliers were blacked out of the published test reports until legislator Charles Mok asked why. They were then published, and the government said future anti-epidemic purchases would go to open tender. This is the course’s theme on corruption and accountability, but we are not alleging corruption. Accountability is what lets the public check value for money afterwards. Here the record arrived late, and only because someone asked.”
