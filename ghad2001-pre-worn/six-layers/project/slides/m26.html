OUR EXTRAPOLATION

Copper fibres in fabric, with breath passing through in seconds, were never tested against the virus on CuMask+.

van Doremalen et al., New England Journal of Medicine 2020 · LCQ15, 20 May 2020

SPEAKER 4 · 8:20–9:15 “Was the copper claim plausible? Partly. In a 2020 laboratory study, the COVID virus stopped being detectable on a copper surface after about four hours, compared with up to three days on plastic. So copper does act on this virus. But a mask is not a hard surface. Breath passes through the fabric in a fraction of a second, and the copper is bonded into fibres. Whether that kills anything in time was never tested on the finished CuMask+, and the government’s own reply says so. That is the difference Park teaches between a plausible idea and a demonstrated effect. A true fact about copper became a claim about a mask that no one had checked.” Confirm the van Doremalen figures (copper about 4 hours, plastic up to 72 hours) before Monday.
