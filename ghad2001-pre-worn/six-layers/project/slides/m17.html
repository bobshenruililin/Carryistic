A mask protects a crowd only if most of the crowd wears it.

THE BODY

Filters particles and bacteria; no copper reached the wearer in tests

THE CROWD

Protection grows with how many people wear one, and how well it fits

THE COST

Harder for deaf people who lip-read; harder to breathe for some; skin problems with long wear

LCQ15 · Government press release, 11 Sep 2020 · Abaluck et al. 2022

SPEAKER 5 · 12:40–13:25 “The immediate health effect has three parts. For the body: it filters particles and bacteria, and tests found no copper reaching the wearer. For the crowd: protection grows with how many people wear masks and how well they fit, so a mask is a collective good, like a vaccine. And the cost: for deaf people who lip-read, for people who struggle to breathe in a mask, for skin worn raw by long hours. Park’s point about equity making collective action work fits here: a free mask for everyone is fairer, and it also protects better.”
