SPEAKER 6 · 12:45–13:45 “Four suggestions, each matched to a gap we found. Test the finished product before making a health claim, and publish the test with the claim. Publish suppliers and prices within weeks, even when buying in an emergency, so that no legislator has to ask. Measure wearing, not just delivery. A simple survey would put a wearing rate beside the 10 million. And plan the end. Copper masks need a take-back route, funded by producers through a textile scheme like the one Europe has just created. Each one adds the missing layer to the record.”
