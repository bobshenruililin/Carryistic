SPEAKER 4 · 9:15–9:50 “And distribution, which answers the brief’s question about shipping. By January 2021, 10.3 million had gone out: 4.1 million by post in the first round, 3.6 million through post offices in the second, 1.7 million through schools, three-quarters of a million through welfare services, and 160,000 at the border. 450,000 were still in stock. Hong Kong can do this in eight months because it is small, dense and has a postal address for almost everyone.”
