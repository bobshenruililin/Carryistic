SPEAKER 6 · 11:45–12:45 “Three conclusions. One: globalisation made even a ‘made in Hong Kong’ mask a global object. Copper from Jerusalem, sewing partly in Vietnam, testing in the United States. The 2020 shortage showed the downside of global supply. CuMask+ showed you still need global supply to replace it. Two: in an emergency, speed traded away scrutiny. There was no open tender, the record came late and only because a legislator asked, and the anti-virus claim was never tested on the finished mask. After February’s queues, the speed was defensible. The missing checks were not. Three: Park’s diffusion lesson. Circulation is not effect. Delivered is not worn, worn is not protected, discarded is not dealt with. The record covered the first step and stopped.”
