Before CuMask+, the people least able to queue went without.

FEBRUARY 2020

Disabled residents described finding masks as “mission impossible”. Some clinics closed.

MAY 2020

Every ID holder except babies; two child sizes; masks sent through schools and care homes

THE PROMOTION

Science, not fashion: “copper capable of immobilising bacteria, common viruses”

AFP / Inquirer, 19 Feb 2020 · Government press release, 5 May 2020

SPEAKER 4 · 10:05–10:55 “The brief asks who is targeted, and how. In February the market targeted whoever could queue longest. Disabled residents called finding masks ‘mission impossible’. CuMask+ targeted everyone. Every ID holder could register, with two children’s sizes, and masks went through schools and care homes so the people least able to queue still got one. That is the commercial determinants idea in reverse: a government using distribution, not advertising, to shape who gets protection. But it was still promoted. The publicity didn’t sell style; it sold science, copper ‘capable of immobilising bacteria and common viruses’. That is the claim we saw was never tested on the finished mask.”
