OUR EXTRAPOLATION

Copper bonded to cotton and plastic layers fits no recycling route in Hong Kong.

Hong Kong makes producers pay for bags, e-waste and glass. Not for textiles.

LCQ19, 13 Jan 2021 · EPD producer responsibility schemes · dashed boxes are our extrapolation

SPEAKER 6 · 14:30–15:25 “Reusable is better than disposable only if it is reused. 10.34 million copper masks were handed out, each good for 60 washes. Nobody recorded how many were worn, how often, or where they are now. Remember the hands raised at the start: that is our only survey, and it suggests many are in drawers. And when they are thrown away, copper bonded to cotton and plastic layers fits no recycling route here. Hong Kong makes producers pay for plastic bags, electronics and glass bottles, but not for textiles. The record stops at the post box, exactly where the environmental question begins.” The dashed boxes are our extrapolation; say so. Count the hands in rehearsal so the number is ready.
