## The story

**Thesis:** A mask made “in Hong Kong” was six layers of globalisation. The record named every maker. It never measured the seventh layer: whether we wore it.

**Criterion:** did circulation become effect? Delivered is not worn, worn is not protected, discarded is not dealt with.

## Design direction

**Already set:**
- **Palette:** graphite #15181C, deep copper #5C2E14, CuMask+ sky #8FCBE5, concrete #C9CED4, paper #F6F7F8, copper #B8642E. These are sampled from the mask itself.
- **Type:** Archivo 700/800 for display, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for labels and the source line, like a care label.
- **Grounds by chapter:** graphite (introduction and conclusion), copper (before), sky (usage), concrete (after).

**Push further.** Apple-keynote restraint, plus the storyboard grammar from Carry’s portfolio work:
- **Window:** a photo inset in a flat field that grows to full bleed.
- **Layer:** photograph, then line drawing, then paint, as evidence moves from seen to inferred. Here the mask’s six layers become the deck’s structure: each chapter peels one off.
- **Echo:** one object repeated to show change.
- **Swatch:** colour sampled from the object.
- **Words as part of the image:** a care-label line, a postmark, a LegCo reference number.

**Engagement beats** (people remember the start, the peak and the end):
1. The show of hands on slide 2, paid off on slide 23.
2. The live teardown on slide 9, with a real mask, counted out loud.
3. The 945 days against 1919 on slide 19.
4. The reveal on slide 27.

**The bench:**
- No cream paper, italic serif accents or accent bars.
- One statement-then-reversal headline at most; three giant-number slides at most.
- One sentence per slide, 24 pt minimum, contrast 4.5:1.
- None of: delve, tapestry, landscape, navigate, journey, unravel.

## Higgsfield

The team workspace had about 35,900 credits on 28 November. Generation works from Claude sessions.

**Rules:**
- Never generate a real place, person, face or event: no queues, no beaches, no factories, no Hong Kong streets.
- The real CuMask+ appears only in real photographs: the group’s photos, or the official image at https://gia.info.gov.hk/general/202005/05/P2020050500692_photo_1172214.jpg.
- Generated material is allowed for:
  - abstract material macros: copper fibre being spun, a meltblown polypropylene web, cotton-viscose weave, light through six translucent layers;
  - transitions between our own photographs;
  - kinetic typography and data;
  - sound.
- Mark anything built on the official photo PRELIMINARY.
- One slow move per film. Export 1920×1080, H.264, under 20 MB, plus a still.

**Shot list:** S1–S6 and data films D1–D3 are specified in `astra-handoff.md`, with models, settings, prompts, what must survive and what rejects a take.

**Run-wild ideas inside the rules:**
1. **A 10-second cold open of materials:** copper fibre spinning → a web of plastic fibres → woven cotton → six layers of light stacking into a silhouette of a mask, with no face. It ends on the title.
2. **Chapter peels:** each chapter card starts as one of the six layers lifting away from the stack.
3. **A quiet score:** one low copper tone that steps up a note per chapter, plus a single paper-tear sound at the teardown.
4. **The seventh layer:** a final slow push from the official three-size photo into empty sky blue, as the closing line lands.
