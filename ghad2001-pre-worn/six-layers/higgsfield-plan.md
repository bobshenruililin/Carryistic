# Six Layers: what Higgsfield does for each slide

**The rule behind every choice.** A camera shows what people saw, so the group's photos carry the evidence. Higgsfield shows what no camera in the room could: copper at fibre scale, the mask's six layers apart, the end of the story before it happened. Every generated frame carries a small mono tag in the corner, `GENERATED ILLUSTRATION`. The evidence-code slide gains a fourth swatch to match: photograph = seen, line = documented, dashed = our extrapolation, tag = illustration, not evidence. Park marks the research, so the deck says which is which.

**References first.** Each plate of the real mask is generated from the government's official product photo (media `3127b468…`), so the cream fabric, fine copper pinstripes, white binding and white ear loops match. These plates are **PRELIMINARY** until the group's Saturday photos replace them or are fed in as better references.

## Sound, used sparingly

- **Cold open cue** (12 s, instrumental): one low tone, then a soft second note as the six layers stack. ElevenLabs Music v2.5, about 2 credits.
- **Chapter steps**: the same tone a note higher at each chapter card (four 3 s stings).
- **Teardown**: the found paper-tear sound.
- **Never**: generated voices. Every voice in the room is a group member, and presentation skills are 15 marks.
- Test the room's speakers on Monday morning. If they are poor, play nothing; the deck works silent.

## Functions considered and set aside

| Function | Why not |
