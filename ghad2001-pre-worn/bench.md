# The bench

The bar for this project only. A slide that fails a line goes back. The four groups follow the marking rubric, then a test for not looking machine-made.

## Brief and timing · 15 marks

- All five sections named on screen in the brief’s words and order: Introduction, Before consumption, Usage & consumption, After consumption, Conclusion & suggestions.
- Every “point to consider” in the brief answered on a named slide (the table on the storyboard page).
- Two timed full run-throughs between 19:00 and 20:30, and a written cut list. Cut the COVID slide first.
- Every member speaks for at least two minutes. Handoffs are scripted.

## Understanding, research, critical engagement · 30 marks

- Every number carries a source code and an evidence medium: photograph (seen), line (documented), paint (our extrapolation), red tab (this exact pair).
- Each chapter has a claim, a mechanism, an example and a qualification.
- At least one course concept per chapter, explained through its mechanism: flows, circulation versus effect, the part and the whole, costs in different accounts, scale mismatch, equity and collective action.
- One counter-argument taken seriously (garment jobs and girls’ schooling) and one company claim tested (“Buy Better, Wear Longer”; the bans).
- Each recommendation names who pays and who checks, and traces back to a finding.
- A real pair inspected, with dated photographs.

## Presentation skills · 15 marks

- One sentence per slide, 20 words at most besides the source line.
- Readable from the back row: body 24 pt or larger, contrast at least 4.5:1.
- Speakers face the room and use cue cards of five lines or fewer.
- An outsider can repeat the thesis and the hole afterwards.
- Each speaker owns two likely questions.

## Not machine-made

- No cream paper, italic serif accents, accent bars or labels above every block.
- The statement-then-reversal headline appears once at most.
- Three giant-number slides at most.
- No generated image of a real place, person or event. AI stand-ins are marked and replaced by Monday.
- No faceless figures seen from behind standing in for people.
- No repeated disclaimers. The evidence key says it once.
- None of: delve, tapestry, landscape, navigate, journey, unravel.
